Recovery math

Calculate a theoretical stake from a payout rate and a chosen recovery amount.

Stake required for break-even: $1.25

At 80% payout, a $1.25 winning stake would earn $1.00 before fees or platform adjustments. A loss of that stake would add $1.25 to the amount you need to recover.

If it wins: session net would be $0.80

This is a scenario calculation, not a forecast. It does not change the trade log.
